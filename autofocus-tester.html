AXIS Autofocus Tester

Sends performAutofocus to /axis-cgi/opticscontrol.cgi (POST/JSON).

Ready. Do step 1 once (logs the browser into the camera), then step 2.

Why two steps?

  1. The camera needs digest auth. Clicking Log in opens http://[ip] so the browser caches your root credentials for that camera.
  2. The autofocus request is then sent fire-and-forget (no-cors). You won't see the JSON reply in the browser, but watch the lens — it will refocus.
  3. For a readable reply, the reliable path is still curl (it does digest + reads the response).
